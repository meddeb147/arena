Privacy

Draft, last updated November 2026. Play credits only. No real money: there are no payments, deposits or withdrawals of any kind.

What we store on your device

Your nickname, a random guest id (so your play-credit wallet survives a page reload), your language, sound settings, personal bests and which how-to cards you have seen. These live in your browser's local storage and never leave it except as described below. Clear your browser data to remove them.

What the game server receives

When you play online: your nickname, the random guest id, the game you join and your in-game inputs. The server keeps your play-credit balance in memory while it runs. We do not ask for your email, name, location or contacts.

Sharing and challenges

"Share & challenge" creates a link that contains your nickname and your score. Only share it with people you are happy to show that to.

Ads, analytics, cookies

There are currently no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If this changes, this page will say so first.

Children

Any future prize-eligible feature will be 18+ only.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/meddeb147/arena.
